# Publishing paths and their manuscript requirements

## Agented (trade)
- **Query letter** (Friedman [S13]): housekeeping (genre/category, word count, title), story description
  (150-300 words), comp titles (recent, not blockbusters or classics), bio (50-100 words, optional for unpublished
  novelists); whole query 200-450 words, one page max; email queries plain text, block style. 80k is the debut
  standard; >120k is "a challenge".
- **Synopsis**: reveals the ending (query does not) [S13].
- **Manuscript format** (Shunn [S14]): white page, 1" margins, black 12-pt standard serif (or Courier); a novel
  needs a separate title page with contact info and "about N words"; page numbering starts at 1 on the first
  text page; each chapter starts on a new page; header Surname / Title / page.
- **Length** (YA fantasy/SF): Laughran (Andrea Brown agency) 50-150k range, sweet spot 65-85k, over 100k "really
  not advisable" for a debut [S11]; Kole: caution above 100k [S10].
- **Timeline after the deal**: ~11-12 months from handover is common; author review windows of 3-4 weeks per
  pass [S12][S25].

## Small press
- Same package as agented, often unagented submission.
- Writer Beware (SFWA) [S17]: small presses have high attrition; check fees, complaints, distribution, how they
  market; beware vanity publishers posing as small presses and misleading terms ("traditional publisher",
  wholesaler vs distributor).
- Red flag [S18]: "How many books are you planning to order?" = back-end vanity. Any author-paid fee for
  editing/publicity/books is a fee to be published.

## Self-publishing
You are the publisher; budget and schedule every service.
- **Edit**: formal copyedit + proofread [S19].
- **ISBN**: one per format (paperback, hardcover, ebook, audiobook); US agency is Bowker (myidentifiers.com) [S20].
  KDP's free ISBN works only on KDP; ebooks may skip an ISBN on KDP; the imprint must match the ISBN record
  [S21].
- **Ebook (KDP)**: reflowable; a logical TOC is required for any book with chapters, HTML TOC strongly
  recommended; every chapter in the TOC with matching names [S22]. EPUB accepted; validate in Kindle Previewer.
  No page numbers/headers in ebooks; title page + copyright in front matter [S22].
- **Print (KDP)** [S23]: common US trim 6x9 (wider than 6.12" or taller than 9" is large trim and costs more);
  minimum outside margin 0.25" (0.375" with bleed); gutter grows with pages: 0.5" for 151-300 pp, 0.625" for
  301-500 pp. A text-only novel needs no interior bleed; covers always need bleed.
- **Print (IngramSpark, for bookstores/libraries)** [S24]: interior PDF separate from cover, single pages, no
  crop marks, all fonts embedded (unembedded = rejected), 0.5" safety margin recommended, PDF/X-1a:2001
  recommended, even page count.
- **Metadata**: KDP reading age 13-17 puts a book in Teen & Young Adult categories [S16].
- **Front/back matter minimum**: title page, copyright page (with ISBN), TOC; optional dedication, glossary/
  pronunciation guide, map, about the author, series teaser.

## Record decisions once
Keep a short "decisions already made (do not re-ask)" list in the project skill: route, shelf/positioning, length
policy, title/series, build tooling, and what is still open (byline, back matter, trim size).
