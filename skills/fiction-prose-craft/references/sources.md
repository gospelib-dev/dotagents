# Sources (verified Nov 2026; secondary summaries named where used)

Primary craft works:
1. Dwight V. Swain, *Techniques of the Selling Writer* (Univ. of Oklahoma Press, 1965). Scene/sequel (ch. 4)
   and motivation-reaction units (ch. 3). Text excerpt seen at bookreadfree.com/247871/6116550.
   Summaries: Wikipedia "Scene and sequel"; September C. Fawkes, "Sequel Structure According to Swain"
   (septembercfawkes.com, 2021).
2. Randy Ingermanson, "Passive Sequels in Fiction Writing" and "Scenes, Sequels, and Chapters"
   (advancedfictionwriting.com, 2009–2011); *Writing Fiction for Dummies* (Wiley, 2009).
3. Jim Butcher, "SEQUELS" and "Putting It All Together" (jimbutcher.livejournal.com/2880.html, /4053.html).
4. Robert McKee, *Story* (HarperCollins, 1997). Scene definition, turning point, "No scene that doesn't
   turn".
5. John Gardner, *The Art of Fiction* (Knopf, 1983). Psychic distance levels; filtering (quoted in Burroway).
6. Emma Darwin, "Psychic Distance: what it is and how to use it" (emmadarwin.typepad.com/thisitchofwriting;
   emmadarwin.substack.com).
7. Janet Burroway, *Writing Fiction: A Guide to Narrative Craft* (many editions). Filtering, quoting Gardner.
   Gardner wording verified via Author magazine, "Fending Off Filters" (authormagazine.org, 2010).
8. Renni Browne & Dave King, *Self-Editing for Fiction Writers*, 2nd ed. (HarperCollins, 2004). R.U.E.;
   explaining dialogue. Dave King interview, Writer Unboxed (2007-07-13).
9. Chuck Palahniuk, "Nuts and Bolts: 'Thought' Verbs" (litreactor.com/essays/chuck-palahniuk/).
10. Ernest Hemingway, *Death in the Afternoon* (Scribner, 1932), ch. 16. Iceberg passage.
11. Anton Chekhov, letter to A. S. Lazarev (Gruzinsky), 1 Nov 1889, via Isaiah Berlin Virtual Library
    (Wikipedia "Chekhov's gun").
12. Brandon Sanderson, "Sanderson's First Law" (brandonsanderson.com, Feb 2007).
13. Brandon Sanderson, BYU *Writing Science Fiction and Fantasy* lectures. 2020 Lecture 2/4 (YouTube;
    notes by Grant Skaggs, grantskaggs.com/posts/sanderson-notes). "Brandon Sanderson's 2025 Guide to Plot
    (Lecture #2)" (brandonsanderson.com blog).
14. Jo Walton, "4th April 2003: Mode" (jowaltonbooks.com). Coins "incluing"; also quoted in Futurismic
    (2010-01-20).
15. Lewis Shiner & Bruce Sterling (eds.), *Turkey City Lexicon: A Primer for SF Workshops*
    (sfwa.org/2009/06/18/turkey-city-lexicon-a-primer-for-sf-workshops/). Uncopyrighted.
16. Les Edgerton, *Hooked* (Writer's Digest Books, 2007). Inciting incident, surface vs story-worthy
    problem. Verified via Writer's Digest excerpt and summaries only: **book text not read directly.**

Unverified / not used: Jack Bickham, *Scene & Structure* (1993). It is known as Swain's student's
elaboration, but I did not verify its content, so no claim here depends on it.
