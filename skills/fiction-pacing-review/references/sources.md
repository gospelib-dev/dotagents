## Evidence cautions

- Craft frameworks are diagnostic traditions, not experimentally proven universal laws.
- The tension and cliffhanger papers support narrow claims stated in the skill; do not generalize beyond their methods.
- Market word-count ranges are practitioner guidance and change over time; verify before submission advice.
- The operational thresholds in `SKILL.md` are local triage heuristics, not source claims.
