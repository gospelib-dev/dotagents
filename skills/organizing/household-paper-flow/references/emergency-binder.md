# In-case-of-emergency binder

## 9. "In case of emergency" binder

Purpose: a grab-and-go folder plus a scanned copy that a spouse, helper or first responder can use in minutes. Build in 2-3 hours; update each January and after major events. Contents follow the ready.gov kit categories: household identification, financial and legal documents, medical information, and household contacts. [ready.gov]

**Section 1 Contacts and plan:** names and numbers of both parents, out-of-state contact, school contacts, neighbors, workplace, doctor, vet, insurers, utilities (water shutoff and gas shutoff locations), banks. Two evacuation routes and meeting points. Register addresses and phones with the county's emergency alert system and save its sender number.

**Section 2 Identification (copies):** photo IDs, birth certificates, SSN cards, passports, military records, pet tags. [ready.gov]

**Section 3 Financial and legal:** policy numbers and agent numbers for home, auto, life, health, flood; bank and card account list (last four digits only); mortgage and loan info; last tax return; will and directive locations; list of digital accounts and where the password manager emergency key is. [ready.gov]

**Section 4 Medical:** medication lists, allergies, immunization records, physicians, insurance cards (copies), power-of-attorney for healthcare. [ready.gov]

**Section 5 Home inventory:** photos or video of each room and valuables, serial numbers, receipts for big items (insurance claims). [ready.gov]

**Storage:** paper in a fire/water-rated box or safe-deposit box, with a second copy with a trusted person; electronic copy encrypted on a USB drive and in a secure cloud. [ready.gov] Keep a go-bag copy small: IDs, contacts, insurance numbers, cash, medications list.

**Update triggers:** January; new insurance; birth, death, marriage; moves; new devices or accounts; a major renovation (update the inventory).

**What goes wrong:** binders made once and never updated; passwords written in the binder (store the password-manager recovery key in the safe, not the whole list); only paper copies in the basement.
