# Selling platforms: fees, fit and effort

2026 figures as found; check each platform's own fee page before quoting.

| Platform | Cost to seller | Best for | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| Facebook Marketplace (local pickup) | $0 local; shipped about 5 percent (min $0.40) per one comparison page (Meta's page did not load; conflicting reports of 10 percent) | Furniture, kids' gear, bulky items | High: messaging, no-shows, scheduling |
| eBay | 13.6 percent final value fee up to $7,500 plus $0.30 per order ($10 or less) or $0.40 (over $10); some categories higher: books/movies/music 15.3 percent, jewelry/watches 15 percent [eBay] | Collectibles, electronics, hobby gear, named brands | Medium-high: photos, listing, shipping, returns |
| Poshmark | $2.95 flat under $15; 20 percent at $15+ (comparison page) | Name-brand adult clothing | Medium: photos and shipping label |
| Mercari | 10 percent (comparison page) | Small goods, toys | Medium |
| Depop | 0 percent commission plus ~3.3 percent and $0.45 processing (comparison page) | Teen/young adult fashion | Medium |
| Etsy | $0.20 listing, 6.5 percent plus ~3 percent processing (comparison page); not for typical household castoffs | Handmade or vintage only | Medium |
| ThredUp or similar clean-out kits | UNCONFIRMED (page blocked); most pay low per-item amounts and reject much | Quality brands in bulk | Low effort, low payout; verify current terms |
| Garage/yard sale | $0 listing | Lots of low-value items in one day | High but time-boxed; a weekend |
