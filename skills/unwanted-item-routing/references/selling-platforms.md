# Selling platforms: how to compare fit, cost and effort

Fees, minimums and payout rules change often and differ by category, so they are never written
here. Look them up at decision time on each platform's own seller-fee page (search "<platform>
seller fees" and open the page on the platform's own domain, e.g. eBay's selling-fees help page or
Etsy's fees and payments policy), and quote them with the date checked. Third-party comparison
pages are a hint only; if the official page will not load, say the figure is unconfirmed.

## Compare on these columns

1. **Net to seller:** expected sale price minus every fee (listing, final value or commission,
   payment processing, per-order fixed fee, shipping label if the seller pays). Work it out for the
   actual item, not a headline percentage; category rates and price tiers often differ.
2. **Fit:** what buyers on that platform actually look for (below).
3. **Effort:** photos, listing time, messaging, shipping or pickup, returns.
4. **Risk:** payment protection, pickup safety, return obligations.

## Durable fit and effort

| Platform type | Best for | Effort |
|---|---|---|
| Local marketplace (pickup) | Furniture, kids' gear, bulky items | High: messaging, no-shows, scheduling |
| Auction / general marketplace (e.g. eBay) | Collectibles, electronics, hobby gear, named brands | Medium-high: photos, listing, shipping, returns |
| Clothing resale apps (e.g. Poshmark, Depop, Mercari) | Name-brand clothing, small goods, toys; each app skews to an age group or style | Medium: photos and a prepaid label |
| Handmade/vintage marketplace (e.g. Etsy) | Handmade or genuinely vintage items only; not typical household castoffs | Medium |
| Clean-out kits / bulk consignment | Quality brands in bulk | Low effort, low payout; many items rejected; read current terms |
| Garage / yard sale | Many low-value items in one day | High but time-boxed |

## Decide

- Under a small value threshold the listing time outweighs the money; donate or bundle instead.
- Prefer the platform with the best net *and* acceptable effort for the person; state both.
- Re-check the fee page if the plan is more than a few weeks old.
